How to get a good enrollment set

  • Sit about an arm's length from the camera so your face fills a good part of the frame.
  • Use the lighting you'll actually use for attendance — avoid a bright window behind you.
  • Click Auto-capture all {{ target }} and follow the on-screen pose prompts — it stops automatically at {{ target }} samples.
  • Blurry, too-small, or multi-face frames are skipped automatically (you'll see why under the counter).
  • When it finishes, go to Students → Train model.
